Guided walkthroughs

What the schema must preserve

  • One Note per owner and Video ID.
  • The Source and Transcript are read-only to the owner.
  • Every content change creates an immutable Revision.
  • The Note points to one Active Revision.
  • The Takeaway is outside Revisions and survives generation.
  • Trash preserves Save time, topic, tags, and history until Purge.
