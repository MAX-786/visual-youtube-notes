# Visual YouTube Notes

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the single Owner, who deliberately saves public YouTube videos and returns later to review what they learned. They use the app frequently on desktop and touch devices. Their main jobs are to scan diagram-first notes, find a remembered idea quickly, make lightweight corrections, and revisit the retained source transcript only when necessary.

## Product Purpose

Visual YouTube Notes turns selected videos into synchronized, searchable learning notes. A useful result makes the main idea visible immediately through a structured Diagram and concise Content blocks, while preserving the private Transcript for grounding, search, regeneration, and occasional verification. Success means an older idea can be found and understood again with little friction.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, focused, and exact. The product should feel like a dependable personal workbench: modern without novelty, calm without becoming empty, and polished without drawing attention away from the Note.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI product styling, purple or blue glow treatments, decorative gradients, stacked cards without hierarchy, oversized marketing typography, cluttered control panels, form-based Diagram editing, and motion that delays work. Do not make the interface feel like a dashboard template or a generated landing page.

## Design Principles

1. Put the learning artifact first. The Diagram and concise Content blocks carry the page; chrome stays secondary.
2. Make retrieval spatial. Search results explain why they matched and open at the matching section or Transcript passage.
3. Edit the thing itself. Text, tables, nodes, and relationships use direct manipulation instead of detached forms or raw payloads.
4. Reveal depth on demand. Transcript, history, cost, and advanced filters remain available through progressive disclosure and lazy loading.
5. Preserve trust. Familiar controls, explicit save state, accessible focus, and predictable light and dark themes make daily use feel safe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus visibility, labeling, and keyboard interaction. Maintain 44px touch targets, support keyboard and touch navigation, never rely on color alone for status, expose Diagram text alternatives, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep both light and dark themes legible.
